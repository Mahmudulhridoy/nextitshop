## Marketplace and Ads: What to Know

Marketplace has its own eligibility rules, and advertising accounts are subject to Meta's advertising policies and spending limits. An account that is "ready" simply has the profile basics in place. Approval and limits are always decided by Meta, so read the Commerce and Advertising policies carefully.

## What to Check Before You Buy

1. 2FA details and the login are included
2. The profile looks complete and normal
3. You can change the email, phone and password
4. A replacement policy is in place

## Set Up Your Account Properly

1. Change the password and add your own recovery details
2. Store the 2FA key in a safe place
3. Review security and login settings
4. Use the account like a real person: add friends, join groups and post naturally
5. Avoid sudden bulk actions on a new login

## A Word on Policy

Meta restricts fake engagement, deceptive behaviour and misuse of multiple accounts. Use each account for a genuine purpose and follow the Community Standards.
