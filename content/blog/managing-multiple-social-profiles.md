One social media profile is a hobby. Five is a job. Agencies, freelancers and growing brands often end up juggling many profiles across Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn, and the cracks appear quickly: forgotten passwords, posts on the wrong page, and nobody sure who owns what.

A little structure fixes most of this.

## 1. Keep a master sheet

Create one spreadsheet with a row for every profile:

- Platform and handle
- Purpose (brand, client, testing)
- Owner on your team
- Recovery email and phone
- Date of last password change

Store it somewhere private and access-controlled, never in a public document.

## 2. Use a password manager

Shared notes and sticky notes lose passwords. A team password manager lets you share access without sharing the actual password, and lets you remove someone the day they leave.

## 3. Separate the roles

Where a platform allows it, add colleagues as **admins, editors or advertisers** on a page rather than handing out the main login. Fewer people holding the master credentials means fewer risks.

## 4. Plan content in one calendar

Pick a single calendar tool and plan a week at a time. For each post, note:

1. The profile it belongs to
2. The goal (awareness, leads, sales)
3. The publish time
4. Who approves it

## 5. Give every profile its own personality

Do not post identical content on all profiles. Adjust the tone, format and hashtags to suit each audience. Platforms also tend to reduce the reach of repetitive, duplicated posts.

## 6. Review monthly

Once a month, check which profiles are growing and which are stalling. Drop or merge the ones that are not earning their keep.

## Final tip

Respect each platform's rules on multiple accounts and automation. Accounts run openly and consistently last longer than anything built around shortcuts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I keep track of many social accounts?

Keep a private master sheet of profiles and owners, and use a team password manager for credentials.

### Should I post the same content on every profile?

No. Adjust tone, format and hashtags for each audience to keep content relevant.

### How often should I review my profiles?

A monthly review is enough to spot which profiles are growing and which need attention.
