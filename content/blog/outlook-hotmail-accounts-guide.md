**Outlook and Hotmail accounts** are Microsoft email accounts that also unlock the wider Microsoft ecosystem: OneDrive storage, Office on the web, Teams and more. If you manage several projects or need separate inboxes, ready-made accounts save time. This guide explains your options and how to set an account up properly.

## Outlook vs Hotmail: What Is the Difference?

Both use the same Microsoft system. Hotmail is the older brand, and Outlook.com is the newer one. Addresses ending in either domain work the same way, and both sign in at outlook.com.

## Why Buy Outlook or Hotmail Accounts?

- **Dedicated inboxes** for different clients, brands or projects
- **Microsoft app access** such as OneDrive, Word, Excel and Teams on the web
- **Reliable delivery** because Outlook is a long-established provider
- **A backup email** for accounts and subscriptions

## Email Client Settings

- **IMAP:** outlook.office365.com, port 993, SSL/TLS
- **SMTP:** smtp-mail.outlook.com, port 587, STARTTLS
- **Username:** the full email address

Microsoft is steadily moving to modern sign-in methods, so older clients may require an update. If a tool cannot sign in, try the web interface first.

## Checklist Before You Buy

1. Check that the seller delivers a working login immediately
2. Look for a clear replacement policy
3. Confirm POP3 or SMTP is available if you need it
4. Make sure you can add your own security info

## Secure Your Account After Delivery

Open **account.microsoft.com/security** and:

1. Change the password
2. Add your own security info (email and phone)
3. Turn on two-step verification
4. Review recent sign-in activity

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a difference between Outlook and Hotmail accounts?

No. They are the same Microsoft system with different domain names.

### Can I connect an Outlook account to an email client?

Yes. Use the IMAP and SMTP settings above, or sign in with your Microsoft account if your client supports it.

### Which is better, fresh or aged?

A fresh account is fine for most tasks. Choose aged if you prefer an account with a longer history.
