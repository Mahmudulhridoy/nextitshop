## First Week Plan

**Day 1:** Change the password, replace the email and phone, turn on 2FA and switch to a Professional account if you are a business.

**Days 2 to 3:** Update the profile photo, bio and link. Post your first piece of content.

**Days 4 to 7:** Post regularly, use relevant hashtags and reply to comments. Follow people in your niche.

## Growth Tips That Work

- Post consistently, because the algorithm favours steady activity
- Use Reels, since they reach beyond your followers
- Reply to comments within the first hour
- Study your Insights to see what works

## Respect Instagram's Rules

Do not buy fake followers, use mass-follow tools or post the same content everywhere. These shortcuts put the account at risk. Read Instagram's Community Guidelines and Terms of Use.
