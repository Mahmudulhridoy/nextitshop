## Connect Yahoo Mail to an Email Client

Use these settings in a client such as Thunderbird or Outlook:

- **Incoming (IMAP):** imap.mail.yahoo.com, port 993, SSL/TLS
- **Outgoing (SMTP):** smtp.mail.yahoo.com, port 465 or 587, SSL/TLS
- **Username:** your full Yahoo address
- **Password:** the app password, not the normal login password
