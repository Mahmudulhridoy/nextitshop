## What Is a Google Voice Account?

Google Voice is a calling and messaging service linked to a Gmail account. It issues a US phone number that works through the web or the mobile app. You can receive calls and texts, forward calls to another phone, read voicemail as text and block spam.

## How to Set Up Your Number

1. **Sign in** at voice.google.com and review the number linked to the account.
2. **Update the recovery details** and enable 2-Step Verification.
3. **Link a phone** to forward calls to your mobile.
4. **Record a voicemail greeting** that states your business name.
5. **Turn on voicemail transcription** so messages arrive as text.
6. **Send a test text and make a test call** to confirm everything works.
