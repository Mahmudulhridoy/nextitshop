If you sell to customers in the United States, a local US number makes you easier to reach and easier to trust. Google Voice gives you one without a new SIM card or a second phone.

## What you can do with Google Voice

- **Make and receive calls** in a browser or on the mobile app
- **Send and receive texts** from the same number
- **Forward calls** to a phone you already carry
- **Read voicemail** as text, so you can scan it in seconds
- **Block spam** with built-in filtering

## Setting up a business-ready line

**1. Choose a number you can remember.** Pick an area code that matches your customers where possible.

**2. Record a clear voicemail greeting.** State your business name and when you usually call back. Keep it under 20 seconds.

**3. Set call forwarding rules.** Send calls to your mobile during working hours and straight to voicemail overnight.

**4. Turn on voicemail transcription.** You will see who called and why without playing every message.

**5. Link it to your Gmail.** Notifications arrive in the inbox you already check.

## Common mistakes

- **Using the number only once and leaving it idle.** Numbers that are never used can be reclaimed. Make a call or send a text from it now and then.
- **Skipping account security.** Your Voice number is tied to your Google account, so turn on 2-Step Verification.
- **Giving the number to everyone.** Use it for customer contact and keep it separate from private accounts.

## Where it fits best

Google Voice suits freelancers, online sellers and small teams who want a US presence with very little setup. For heavy call volume or many staff members, a dedicated business phone system is a better fit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I make calls with Google Voice?

Yes. You can place and receive calls through the web or the mobile app.

### Why do I need to use the number regularly?

Numbers that stay unused for a long period can be reclaimed, so occasional calls or texts keep it active.

### Can Google Voice forward calls to my mobile?

Yes. Add your phone number in the settings and choose when calls should forward.
