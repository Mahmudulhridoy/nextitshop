## Make the Profile Yours

1. Change the password and security details
2. Update the headline, summary and experience to match your real role
3. Add a professional photo and banner

## Outreach the Right Way

LinkedIn is strict about automation and spam. Send personalised connection requests, keep messages short and relevant, and avoid mass copy-paste outreach. Never use scraping tools that break the LinkedIn User Agreement. Quality conversations will always outperform volume.
