Discord has grown from a gaming chat tool into the default home for communities, creators, crypto projects and software teams. Many servers ask new members to verify an email and a phone number before they can speak. This guide explains the **Discord account** options and how to use one responsibly.

## What Is a Verified Discord Account?

A verified Discord account has a confirmed email address and a linked phone number. Many servers set a minimum verification level so that new, unverified members cannot post straight away. A verified account clears that hurdle.

## Who Uses Extra Discord Accounts?

- **Community managers** who keep an admin account and a moderator account
- **Developers** testing bots, webhooks and server integrations
- **Support teams** staffing a help server
- **Creators and brands** with a different account for each community
- **Gamers** wanting a separate identity for different games or teams

## Set Up Your Account Safely

- Change the password and email to your own
- Turn on two-factor authentication
- Customise the avatar, banner and display name
- Read each server's rules before you post
- Never share your token with anyone. It works like a password.

## Use Discord the Right Way

Join servers because you are interested in them, take part in conversations and stay within Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Mass joining, unsolicited direct messages and spam are the fastest ways to lose an account.
